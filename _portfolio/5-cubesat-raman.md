### CubeSat imaging for hypersonic vehicle tracking
**Tarleton TexanSat Laboratory, August 2024–May 2025.** Advisor: Dr. Rafael Landaeta

- Designed a CubeSat imaging system to detect and track hypersonic vehicles from low Earth orbit by their thermal signature
- Used Wien's law and vehicle temperature data to identify a 0.4–2.1 µm peak emission band, which drove the choice of a silicon VSWIR imager targeting the hotter, faster flight phases
- Modeled CMOS sensor response and compared sensor and lens configurations for long-range detection
- Published at the [2025 AIAA Regional Student Conference](https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2025-97548)

### Low-cost Raman spectrometer
**Tarleton Optics Lab, September 2024–May 2025.** Advisor: Dr. Christopher Marble

- Built a low-cost Raman spectrometer for liquid samples with 0.5 nm FWHM resolution
- Wrote a Python calibration pipeline that matches measured neon lines to NIST reference data
- Presented at the Texas Section AAPT Joint Spring Meeting, April 2025

A CubeSat imaging system for detecting hypersonic vehicles from low Earth orbit by their thermal signature. Using Wien's law and vehicle temperature data, we identified a 0.4–2.1 µm peak emission band and chose a silicon (VSWIR) imager to target the shorter wavelengths emitted during hotter, faster flight phases.
